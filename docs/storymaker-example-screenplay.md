# Example: Storymaker-Optimized Screenplay

This example demonstrates proper formatting and production-note detail for Storymaker import.

---

## Story Metadata

```
TITLE: THE ARCHIVIST
LOGLINE: A librarian discovers her estranged mother's love letters in the restricted archives and must choose between honoring family secrets or revealing the truth that could heal her own life.
GENRE: Intimate Drama, Coming-of-Age
THEMES: Secrets and truth, legacy and identity, the burden of silence, healing through understanding
TONE: Contemplative with moments of quiet joy and painful recognition
TARGET AUDIENCE: Adults 25-55, literary drama audiences
```

---

## Characters

```
CHARACTER: MAYA CHEN
ARCHETYPE: The Seeker / Protagonist
AGE/TYPE: 32, Asian-American, librarian
APPEARANCE: Long black hair usually in a messy bun, oversized cardigans over vintage band t-shirts, wire-rimmed glasses she's always pushing up. Tall, slender build, moves with careful deliberation. No makeup except coffee stains on her sleeve.
MOTIVATION: Seeking to understand her mother's past and, by extension, her own identity. Wants to know if her mother was happy.
ACTING NOTES: Naturally curious but cautious. Speaks in measured tones. Physical hesitation before moments of emotion—a held breath before confession. Dry, self-deprecating humor masking deeper wounds.
```

```
CHARACTER: RICHARD CHEN (Father)
ARCHETYPE: The Guardian (of secrets)
AGE/TYPE: 68, Maya's father, retired engineer
APPEARANCE: Silver-haired, tailored polo shirts, reading glasses on a chain. Neat beard, tired eyes that suggest years of unspoken things. Deliberate posture, formal carriage.
MOTIVATION: Protecting his family's reputation and his daughter's image of her mother. Fears what the truth might destroy.
ACTING NOTES: Reserved, intellectual. Chooses words carefully. Long silences before speaking. Shows love through acts rather than words. Becomes defensive when pressed.
```

```
CHARACTER: ELENA MORETTI
ARCHETYPE: The Mentor / Antagonist's perspective
AGE/TYPE: 84, retired architect, her mother's closest friend
APPEARANCE: Petite, Italian, silver curls, always wears vintage dresses and bright scarves. Gnarled hands that gesture expressively. Clear, sharp eyes that miss nothing. Moves slowly but with dignity.
MOTIVATION: Believes Maya deserves to know the truth. Wants to honor her late friend's memory authentically. Guilty about decades of silence.
ACTING NOTES: Warm and forthright, no filter. Laughs easily at life's absurdities. Switches between firmness and vulnerability when discussing the past. Speaks with poetic, old-world rhythm.
```

## Scene Breakdown

### SCENE 1: INT. UNIVERSITY ARCHIVE BASEMENT - MORNING (PRESENT DAY)

**LOGLINE:** Maya discovers her late mother's letters tucked behind old photograph albums in the restricted archives.

**SETTING:** A narrow basement corridor with tall steel shelving units housing bound archives, boxes, and indexed filing systems. Fluorescent lights hum overhead, casting a pale greenish glow. A vintage rolling ladder leans against one wall. Dust particles float in thin horizontal shafts of light from a high ground-level window. The air smells of old paper, preservation chemicals, and decades of quiet.

**ATMOSPHERE:** Quiet dread. Sacred and secret. The intimacy of undisturbed spaces. A sense that this basement holds truths.

**CHARACTER(S):** Maya (alone)

**ACTION:**
Maya descends the basement stairs with a cardboard box, checking her phone: 7:47 AM. The archive doesn't open until 9. She approaches shelving unit F-7, marked with faded labels: "PERSONAL COLLECTIONS - RESTRICTED." She reaches for a bound album at shoulder height labeled "CHEN FAMILY - 1985-1995." Behind it, her fingers brush something else—a weathered manila envelope, hand-labeled in her mother's handwriting: "E.M. - KEEP SAFE."

Her breath catches. She pulls it down slowly.

Inside: forty-three letters, handwritten, chronological, dated 1978-1992. The letterhead is architecture firm. The signature: Elena Moretti.

Maya sinks onto the basement steps, envelope in lap, unable to move.

**DIALOGUE:**
(None - internal moment)

**PRODUCTION NOTES:**
- **CAMERA:** Static wide shot of basement from top of stairs as Maya enters (establishing loneliness of space). Slow push-in as she approaches shelf. Extreme close-up of hands discovering envelope (trembling). Handheld shaky as she sits, capturing disorientation.
- **LIGHTING:** Harsh fluorescent overhead creating institutional dread. High-contrast shadows under shelving. Single shaft of morning sunlight through high window hitting the envelope as it's pulled down (subtle divine touch).
- **PERFORMANCE:** Maya's movements deliberate and careful at first, then freezing. Shock on her face—not tears yet, just numbness. Her hand shakes as she holds the envelope. She looks at her phone (checking time as grounding ritual) before ascending into her discovery.
- **AUDIO:** Fluorescent hum (constant tension). Her footsteps echo. Envelope paper rustles. Her breathing becomes audible as she sits. No music—silence amplifies the moment.
- **VFX/EFFECTS:** Light dust disturbed as she reaches for album. Envelope and letters treated as sacred objects.
- **CONTINUITY:** Maya wears her archive ID and keys. The time (early morning) explains why she's alone. She'll carry this envelope through the rest of the story.

**SHOTS:**
- SHOT 1: Wide establishing of basement—narrow, institutional, isolated
- SHOT 2: Maya descending stairs, box in arms, looking at phone
- SHOT 3: Close on shelving labels, her hand reaching
- SHOT 4: Extreme close-up: envelope pulled from behind album
- SHOT 5: Close on handwriting: "E.M. - KEEP SAFE"
- SHOT 6: Medium shot of Maya sitting on stairs, envelope in hands, shock registering
- SHOT 7: Close-up of letters inside envelope, first one dated "February 14, 1978"

---

### SCENE 2: INT. MAYA'S APARTMENT - LIVING ROOM - AFTERNOON (SAME DAY)

**LOGLINE:** Maya reads the first letter and realizes her mother had a secret love affair with Elena Moretti.

**SETTING:** A cluttered but lived-in one-bedroom. Books stacked everywhere—on shelves, windowsills, under the couch. Vinyl records and a turntable. Mismatched furniture showing years of college-to-adulthood acquisition. A window overlooking a quiet residential street. Late afternoon light slants golden through gauzy curtains.

**ATMOSPHERE:** Intimate, vulnerable, warm despite the chaos. The space of someone who lives in ideas and the past.

**CHARACTER(S):** Maya (alone)

**ACTION:**
Maya sits cross-legged on her couch, curled into the corner. She has changed into comfortable clothes (sweatpants, college sweatshirt) and made tea that sits untouched beside her. The envelope is open, letters spread across her lap like tarot cards.

She reads the first letter aloud, voice wavering:

**DIALOGUE:**
**MAYA**
"My dearest Margaret—February 14, 1978. I don't know why I'm writing this. I know you're sleeping in the next room, your husband snoring like a happy seal. Your daughter—our daughter, in my heart—is asleep in her crib. And I'm awake, burning, because tomorrow I board a plane to Rome and I don't know if I can do this. Pretend. Keep pretending. Elena."

(She stops, sets the letter down. Then picks it up again, rereading the line: "our daughter, in my heart.")

**PRODUCTION NOTES:**
- **CAMERA:** Static two-shot of Maya on couch from across the room, giving us space and isolation. Slow push-in as she begins reading. Extreme close-up of hands holding letter. Final shot pulls back slowly as she absorbs meaning.
- **LIGHTING:** Golden afternoon through curtains creating warm but slightly melancholic tone. Lamp light on her face from side angle (half-lit, symbolizing revelation). Books cast long shadows creating visual complexity/interiority.
- **PERFORMANCE:** Maya is still in shock—reading mechanically at first, then emotion builds. Her voice cracks on "our daughter." She touches the words with her finger, tracing handwriting. Hands trembling visibly.
- **AUDIO:** Muted ambient of city through window (distant traffic, life continuing). Her breathing. Page rustling. Quiet tea-sip. Her voice reading aloud is central—vulnerable, unfiltered. No music.
- **VFX/EFFECTS:** Tea steam from mug. Dust particles in afternoon light. Books slightly askew, suggesting earlier agitation.
- **CONTINUITY:** Maya still wears the archive ID around her neck. Envelope is now opening—this is the threshold moment.

**SHOTS:**
- SHOT 1: Wide of Maya on couch in apartment, books surrounding, isolated
- SHOT 2: Medium from across room as she opens first letter
- SHOT 3: Close on her face as she begins reading, confusion turning to shock
- SHOT 4: Extreme close on hands holding letter, trembling
- SHOT 5: Close on the words of the letter as she reads
- SHOT 6: Medium on face as voice cracks on "our daughter"
- SHOT 7: Pull back to wide showing her curled smaller on the couch, overwhelmed

---

### SCENE 3: INT. ELENA'S APARTMENT - KITCHEN - EVENING

**LOGLINE:** Maya confronts Elena with the letters. Elena confirms the truth and reveals the depth of the relationship.

**SETTING:** A bright, airy upper-floor apartment in an older building with tall windows framing the city sunset. The kitchen opens to a living room filled with architectural drawings, awards, and photographs spanning 50+ years. Bookshelves packed with design books and framed photos of Elena with significant figures. A marble counter where Elena has been preparing dinner.

**ATMOSPHERE:** Cultured, openly lived, reflective of a life well-chronicled. Warm and unapologetically intimate (photos of Elena and women together, travel, joy).

**CHARACTER(S):** Maya, Elena

**ACTION:**
Maya enters with the envelope, angry and trembling. Elena is setting a table—she stops mid-motion when she sees the envelope. The gesture is instantly recognizable: confirmation.

Maya doesn't sit. She stands in the kitchen entryway, envelope held like an accusation.

**DIALOGUE:**
**MAYA**
You knew. You knew my entire life and you never—

**ELENA**
(Quietly)
Yes.

**MAYA**
You just... "yes"? Forty-three letters and you say "yes"?

**ELENA**
(Setting down the plate she's holding)
Sit. I'll make tea. We have forty years to discuss. Tea seems appropriate.

**MAYA**
I don't want tea. I want to know if this is real. If she... if you...

**ELENA**
(Turning to face her directly)
Your mother was the love of my life. I was the love of hers. We lived in a time and a world that did not allow us to say so. So we wrote letters instead. We took two weeks every other year—architecture conferences, your mother's "work trips." We had those stolen weeks, and we had these letters. And we had a daughter, though she didn't know it was ours. Not in that way.

(Pause. Elena studies Maya's face.)

You look like her when she was angry. Same jaw. Same way of holding your shoulders.

**PRODUCTION NOTES:**
- **CAMERA:** Medium shot of Maya in doorway (entrance into truth). Static shot of Elena as she turns and faces Maya directly (moment of facing). Two-shot favoring Elena's face as she speaks (her composure contrasted with Maya's agitation). Final close-up on Elena's face as she observes Maya's resemblance to her mother.
- **LIGHTING:** Sunset flooding through windows (golden, warm, illuminating the truth as beautiful). Elena's face half-lit, half in shadow (duality of hidden life). Maya remains in kitchen shadow initially, stepping into light as she accepts the truth.
- **PERFORMANCE:** Maya is brittle, anger-armed against vulnerability. Elena is calm, undefensive, almost relieved to finally speak. She moves slowly, deliberately. When she turns to face Maya, it's a moment of courage—50+ years of held truth releasing.
- **AUDIO:** City sounds through window (sunset traffic). Plate set gently on table (grounding moment). Elena's voice is measured and clear. No music. Ambient silence allows emotion to breathe.
- **VFX/EFFECTS:** Sunset light creates warm color shift as scene progresses. Architectural drawings on walls visible in background—Elena's creative legacy.
- **CONTINUITY:** Maya still holding envelope. Elena wears a simple dress, no makeup needed. The kitchen table is half-set (interrupted by revelation).

**SHOTS:**
- SHOT 1: Wide establishing of kitchen/living room showing photographs and Elena's collected life on walls
- SHOT 2: Medium of Elena mid-motion, then freezing as Maya enters
- SHOT 3: Two-shot from the side—Maya in doorway, Elena by counter, distance between them
- SHOT 4: Close on the envelope in Maya's hands
- SHOT 5: Close on Elena's face as she says "Yes"
- SHOT 6: Medium of Elena setting down plate (composed action contrasting internal truth)
- SHOT 7: Two-shot as Elena turns to face Maya fully
- SHOT 8: Close on Elena's eyes as she studies Maya's face
- SHOT 9: Over-the-shoulder shot of Maya from Elena's perspective, seeing the resemblance
- SHOT 10: Final close on both faces—Elena's peace, Maya's dawning understanding

## Character Dialogue Reference

This screenplay demonstrates:
- **Natural, distinct voices** (Elena speaks formally/poetically; Maya is academic/careful)
- **Subtext-heavy exchanges** (what's not said matters as much as dialogue)
- **Dialogue that advances plot and reveals character** (Elena's calmness reveals her acceptance; Maya's anger reveals fear)

## Production Notes Master Reference

This screenplay example includes detailed notes for:
- **CAMERA:** Static establishes. Push-ins for emotional intensity. Close-ups for hands/faces revealing feeling. Pull-backs for realization.
- **LIGHTING:** Fluorescent for institutional dread. Golden sunset for revelation as beautiful. Side-lighting for moral ambiguity.
- **PERFORMANCE:** Trembling as physicalization of shock. Measured speech as emotional control. Speed changes with emotional state.
- **AUDIO:** Silence as tension. Ambient grounding. Voice-reading creating vulnerability.
- **VFX/EFFECTS:** Minimal but symbolic (dust, steam, light)
- **CONTINUITY:** Envelope as talisman. Archive ID reminder of story origin. Time progression implicit.

## How Storymaker Processes This Screenplay

When imported:
1. **Parses logline** → Creates project premise
2. **Extracts characters** → Populates character Bible with appearance + motivation
3. **Identifies production signals** → "Trembling hands," "golden sunset," "tears," "frozen in shock" inform image generation prompts
4. **Detects camera/lighting notes** → Directs AI image generation composition
5. **Breaks scenes into shots** → Autogenerates shot-level storyboard structure
6. **Embeds dialogue + action** → Populates text/SFX layers for each shot

This screenplay is optimized because:
- ✓ Production notes are specific to visual generation
- ✓ Character appearances are "what camera sees," not internal traits
- ✓ Emotional beats are physicalized (trembling, breathing, etc.)
- ✓ Shots are pre-broken into frameable units
- ✓ Lighting and camera are explicit, not suggestive
