# Common Formatting Mistakes & Best Practices

This guide identifies errors that will degrade Storymaker imports and shows the correct approach.

---

## ❌ LOGLINE Mistakes

### ❌ Too Vague
```
LOGLINE: A woman finds letters and learns a secret.
```
**Problem:** Could describe 1,000 stories. No emotional stakes, no conflict clarity.

### ✓ Correct
```
LOGLINE: A librarian discovers her late mother's love letters and must choose between honoring family secrets or revealing the truth that could heal her own life.
```
**Why it works:** Specific character, clear goal, stated stakes, emotional conflict.

---

### ❌ Too Long (Becomes Summary)
```
LOGLINE: A young archivist named Maya Chen, who has always felt disconnected from her estranged mother, discovers love letters between her mother and Elena Moretti, a renowned architect. As she reads through their correspondence spanning 15 years, she learns that her mother had a secret life that her father kept hidden. Maya must decide whether to confront her father, reach out to Elena, or keep the secret to preserve her family's reputation, all while grappling with her own identity and sense of belonging.
```
**Problem:** This is a synopsis, not a logline. Too much detail, loses emotional punch.

### ✓ Correct
```
LOGLINE: A librarian discovers her late mother's love letters and must choose between honoring family secrets or revealing the truth that could heal her own life.
```
**Why it works:** All essential tension in 2 sentences.

---

### ❌ Lacks Conflict/Stakes
```
LOGLINE: A woman inherits her mother's house and discovers old letters.
```
**Problem:** No external goal, no stakes, no emotional core.

### ✓ Correct
```
LOGLINE: When a woman inherits her mother's house, she discovers love letters revealing a decades-long affair—and must decide whether to expose the truth or protect the family she thought she knew.
```
**Why it works:** Clear conflict (discovery forces choice), emotional stakes (family trust).

## ❌ CHARACTER Mistakes

### ❌ Only Internal Traits (Not Filmable)
```
CHARACTER: MAYA
APPEARANCE: Intelligent, thoughtful, introspective
MOTIVATION: To find herself
ACTING NOTES: Reflective, processing her emotions
```
**Problem:** Nothing a camera can see or an actor can play. "Intelligent" and "thoughtful" are invisible.

### ✓ Correct
```
CHARACTER: MAYA
APPEARANCE: 32, Asian-American librarian, oversized cardigans, wire-rimmed glasses perpetually slipping down, hair in messy bun, no makeup. Moves with careful deliberation. Coffee stains on her sleeves are common.
MOTIVATION: Seeking to understand her mother's past and, by extension, her own identity.
ACTING NOTES: Naturally curious but cautious. Speaks in measured tones. Physical hesitation before emotional moments—a held breath before confession. Dry, self-deprecating humor masking deeper wounds.
```
**Why it works:** Specific visual details, emotionally grounded acting choices, filmable quirks.

---

### ❌ Too Generic
```
CHARACTER: ELENA
APPEARANCE: Old woman, kind, wise
MOTIVATION: Wants to help
ACTING NOTES: Warm and supportive
```
**Problem:** Could describe any elderly mentor character. No specificity.

### ✓ Correct
```
CHARACTER: ELENA MORETTI
APPEARANCE: 84, Italian architect, silver curls, always wears vintage dresses with bright scarves. Gnarled hands that gesture expressively. Clear, sharp eyes that miss nothing. Moves slowly but with dignity. Petite frame.
MOTIVATION: Believes Maya deserves truth. Wants to honor her late friend's authentic memory. Carries guilt about decades of silence.
ACTING NOTES: Warm and forthright, no filter. Laughs easily at life's absurdities. Switches between firmness and vulnerability when discussing the past. Speaks with poetic, old-world rhythm. Treats conversation like a sacred act.
```
**Why it works:** Specific age, ethnicity, style. Distinct physical vocabulary. Emotional complexity in motivation.

---

### ❌ No Performance Direction
```
CHARACTER: JAMES
APPEARANCE: 50s, businessman
MOTIVATION: To win the deal
ACTING NOTES: Determined
```
**Problem:** "Determined" is generic. How does a camera capture determination? No distinct voice/physicality.

### ✓ Correct
```
CHARACTER: JAMES BERKLEY
APPEARANCE: 50s, sharp tailored suits, thinning hair carefully combed-over, Rolex visible, reading glasses on a chain. Perpetually checking his phone. Firm handshake. Taut jaw.
MOTIVATION: To secure the deal before his company is acquired. To prove he still has value.
ACTING NOTES: Impatient and controlling. Speaks quickly, finishing others' sentences. Fidgets constantly—tapping pen, adjusting cufflinks. Forced smile when performing confidence. Real smile only when talking about his late mentor. Chooses words like a lawyer, calculating each phrase for impact.
```
**Why it works:** Visual appearance + behavioral tics + vocal choice + emotional subtext. Filmable.

## ❌ SCENE STRUCTURE Mistakes

### ❌ No Setting Description
```
SCENE 1: INT. OFFICE - MORNING
ACTION: Maya sits at her desk and reads the letter.
DIALOGUE: [none]
```
**Problem:** "Office" could be anyone's office. No mood, no atmosphere, no visual identity.

### ✓ Correct
```
SCENE 1: INT. UNIVERSITY ARCHIVE BASEMENT - MORNING
SETTING: A narrow basement corridor with tall steel shelving units housing bound archives. Fluorescent lights hum overhead casting pale greenish glow. Dust particles float in thin shafts of light from high ground-level window. Air smells of old paper, preservation chemicals, and decades of quiet.
ATMOSPHERE: Quiet dread. Sacred and secret. Intimacy of undisturbed spaces.
```
**Why it works:** Specific location, sensory details, mood/atmosphere established.

---

### ❌ Vague Action Description
```
ACTION: Maya enters the room and finds the letters. She is shocked.
```
**Problem:** "Shocked" is telling, not showing. No visual action, no camera guidance.

### ✓ Correct
```
ACTION: Maya descends the basement stairs with a cardboard box, checking her phone: 7:47 AM. She approaches shelving unit F-7, marked "PERSONAL COLLECTIONS - RESTRICTED." She reaches for a bound album at shoulder height. Behind it, her fingers brush something else—a weathered manila envelope, hand-labeled in her mother's handwriting: "E.M. - KEEP SAFE." Her breath catches. She pulls it down slowly. Inside: forty-three letters, handwritten, chronological.
```
**Why it works:** Specific actions, visual details, behavior reveals emotion (checking phone as grounding, breath catching as shock).

---

### ❌ Production Notes Too Generic
```
PRODUCTION NOTES:
- CAMERA: Good framing
- LIGHTING: Dramatic
- PERFORMANCE: Emotional
- AUDIO: Background sound
- VFX/EFFECTS: None
- CONTINUITY: She has the envelope
```
**Problem:** "Good framing," "dramatic," "emotional" are not direction. AI won't know what to generate.

### ✓ Correct
```
PRODUCTION NOTES:
- CAMERA: Static wide shot from top of stairs as Maya enters (establishing loneliness). Slow push-in as she approaches shelf. Extreme close-up of hands discovering envelope (trembling). Handheld shaky as she sits, capturing disorientation.
- LIGHTING: Harsh fluorescent overhead creating institutional dread. High-contrast shadows under shelving. Single shaft of morning sunlight through high window hitting the envelope as it's pulled down (subtle divine touch).
- PERFORMANCE: Maya's movements deliberate and careful at first, then freezing. Shock registering on her face—not tears yet, just numbness. Her hand shakes as she holds the envelope. She looks at her phone (checking time as grounding ritual) before ascending into discovery.
- AUDIO: Fluorescent hum (constant tension). Her footsteps echo. Envelope paper rustles. Her breathing becomes audible as she sits.
- VFX/EFFECTS: Light dust disturbed as she reaches. Envelope and letters treated as sacred objects.
- CONTINUITY: Maya wears her archive ID and keys. The time (early morning) explains why she's alone. She'll carry this envelope through the rest of the story.
```
**Why it works:** Specific camera movement, exact lighting mood + technique, physicalized emotion, audio choices + purpose.

---

### ❌ Dialogue Too Expository
```
MAYA: I can't believe you've been my mother's lover for all these years and no one ever told me!
```
**Problem:** Characters don't naturally exposit. This would never be said this way in real life.

### ✓ Correct
```
MAYA: You knew. You knew my entire life and you never—

ELENA: Yes.

MAYA: You just... "yes"? Forty-three letters and you say "yes"?
```
**Why it works:** Natural speech pattern, subtext-heavy, dialogue reveals rather than explains.

---

### ❌ Shots Not Specified
```
PRODUCTION NOTES: ...
(No shot breakdown)
```
**Problem:** Storymaker needs shot-level clarity to build storyboard. Vague about framing.

### ✓ Correct
```
SHOTS:
- SHOT 1: Wide establishing of basement—narrow, institutional, isolated
- SHOT 2: Maya descending stairs, box in arms, looking at phone
- SHOT 3: Close on shelving labels, her hand reaching
- SHOT 4: Extreme close-up: envelope pulled from behind album
- SHOT 5: Close on handwriting: "E.M. - KEEP SAFE"
- SHOT 6: Medium shot of Maya sitting on stairs, envelope in hands, shock registering
- SHOT 7: Close-up of letters inside envelope, first one dated "February 14, 1978"
```
**Why it works:** Clear shot progression, specific framing, builds visual narrative.

## ❌ TONE & CONSISTENCY Mistakes

### ❌ Tone Shifts Unexplained
```
SCENE 1: Intimate, vulnerable drama. [Production notes match]
SCENE 2: Action-packed chase through warehouse. [Sudden genre shift]
SCENE 3: Back to intimate drama.
```
**Problem:** Jarring shifts without earned narrative justification. Confuses AI generation and story flow.

### ✓ Correct
```
SCENE 1: Intimate discovery in quiet archive. [Contemplative, still camera]
SCENE 2: Confrontation in Elena's apartment. [More dynamic camera, emotional escalation]
SCENE 3: Vulnerable conversation on Elena's couch. [Return to stillness, now earned]
```
**Why it works:** Tone evolves with emotional intensity, not arbitrarily. Progression feels intentional.

---

### ❌ Inconsistent Character Voice
```
MAYA (Scene 1): "One must endeavor to contemplate the epistemological ramifications of maternal obscurity."

MAYA (Scene 2): "Ugh, like, my mom was totally lying to me, you know?"

MAYA (Scene 3): [Back to overly formal speech]
```
**Problem:** Character has no consistent voice. Actor would be confused.

### ✓ Correct
```
MAYA (Scene 1): "I found letters. Old letters in the archive. Letters my mother wrote."

MAYA (Scene 2): "You knew? You knew and you never—" [Emotional, fragmented]

MAYA (Scene 3): "I need to understand what this means. For me. For my family."
```
**Why it works:** Consistent voice (careful, measured) across scenes. Intensity shifts with emotion, not character inconsistency.

## ❌ PRODUCTION NOTES Mistakes

### ❌ Contradictory Notes
```
CAMERA: Static shot [no movement]
But also: Handheld shaky cam following through the space
```
**Problem:** Incompatible camera styles in same scene. AI can't reconcile.

### ✓ Correct
```
CAMERA: Static wide establishing shot. Then transition to close-up as emotion intensifies. Final shot is slow push-in capturing the moment of realization.
```
**Why it works:** Clear progression of camera technique matching emotional arc.

---

### ❌ Overly Technical (Confusing Direction)
```
LIGHTING: f/2.8 aperture, 58mm prime lens, 5600K color temperature, -2 exposure compensation with 3-stop ND filter
```
**Problem:** This is cinematography-specific jargon, not lighting description. Storymaker needs mood/visual direction, not specs.

### ✓ Correct
```
LIGHTING: Harsh overhead fluorescent creating institutional dread. High-contrast shadows emphasizing isolation. Single shaft of warm morning sunlight through window as counterpoint—subtle hope.
```
**Why it works:** Describes WHAT camera will see and WHY, not technical specs.

---

### ❌ Insufficient Production Notes
```
PRODUCTION NOTES:
- CAMERA: Two-shot
- LIGHTING: Interior
- PERFORMANCE: Emotional
- AUDIO: Dialogue
- VFX/EFFECTS: None
- CONTINUITY: Characters present from last scene
```
**Problem:** Each note is one word. Storymaker's AI has no direction. All generic.

### ✓ Correct
```
PRODUCTION NOTES:
- CAMERA: Two-shot from side angle, Elena on left, Maya on right, distance between them. Slow push-in as Elena begins speaking, tightening the frame and their emotional connection.
- LIGHTING: Sunset golden light through windows, warm and illuminating. Elena's face half-lit, half in shadow (duality of hidden life finally revealed). Maya in kitchen shadow initially, stepping into light as she accepts truth.
- PERFORMANCE: Maya is brittle, anger-armed against vulnerability. Elena is calm, undefensive, almost relieved to finally speak after 50+ years of silence. She moves slowly, deliberately. When she turns to face Maya, it's courage visibly summoned.
- AUDIO: City sounds through window (sunset traffic faint). Elena's voice measured and clear. No music. Silence allows emotion to breathe. Plate set gently on table as grounding moment.
- VFX/EFFECTS: Sunset light creates warm color shift as scene progresses. Architectural drawings on walls visible in background (visual legacy).
- CONTINUITY: Maya still holding envelope. Elena wears simple dress. Kitchen table half-set (interrupted by revelation).
```
**Why it works:** Every note gives direction and reasoning. Storymaker knows exactly what to generate.

## ✓ BEST PRACTICES Checklist

Before submitting screenplay to Storymaker:

### Structure
- [ ] Logline is 1-2 sentences, specific to story, clear conflict + stakes
- [ ] 3+ characters with appearance (visual traits), motivation, and acting notes
- [ ] Scenes numbered, location formatted (INT/EXT - LOCATION - TIME)
- [ ] Every scene has: Setting, Atmosphere, Character list, Action, Dialogue, Production Notes, Shots

### Production Notes
- [ ] CAMERA: Specific movement + framing (not "good shot")
- [ ] LIGHTING: Time of day OR source + mood (not "dramatic lighting")
- [ ] PERFORMANCE: Physicalized emotion + vocal choice (not just "emotional")
- [ ] AUDIO: Ambient, diegetic, or silence-as-choice (not missing)
- [ ] VFX/EFFECTS: Visual elements or "none required" (not blank)
- [ ] CONTINUITY: Carry-forward from prior scene (not forgotten)

### Dialogue
- [ ] Natural speech patterns (not exposition)
- [ ] Character-distinct voices (not all sound the same)
- [ ] Subtext evident (characters don't say everything)
- [ ] Advances plot or reveals character (not filler)

### Tone & Consistency
- [ ] Consistent character voices across scenes (same actor would recognize self)
- [ ] Tone evolves with emotional intensity (intentional, not random)
- [ ] Themes reinforced through visual language (lighting, camera, production notes)
- [ ] No unexplained genre shifts (transitions are earned)

### Visual Clarity
- [ ] Shots broken down (5-7 per scene minimum)
- [ ] Specific framing language (over-the-shoulder, close-up, wide, etc.)
- [ ] Lighting mood is filmable (not abstract)
- [ ] Camera movement has purpose tied to emotion

### Length & Pacing
- [ ] 1 page ≈ 1 minute screen time (judge total runtime)
- [ ] Scenes escalate in emotional intensity (peaks and valleys)
- [ ] No unnecessary exposition (every scene advances story)
- [ ] Run time is realistic for story scope

## Common AI Generation Failures (How to Avoid)

### ❌ Generated Image: Blurry, unclear, unrelated to scene
**Cause:** Vague production notes. "Emotional lighting" doesn't specify what camera sees.
**Fix:** "Harsh overhead fluorescent with shadows under eyes, creating institutional dread"

### ❌ Generated Image: Wrong tone or mood
**Cause:** Lighting notes don't match scene emotion.
**Fix:** Specify color temperature + mood: "Cool blue moonlight suggesting cold isolation"

### ❌ Generated Shot: Wrong framing
**Cause:** Camera direction missing or vague.
**Fix:** Specify: "Close-up on hands trembling, filling 60% of frame"

### ❌ Generated Character: Doesn't look like character described
**Cause:** Appearance notes are internal traits, not visual.
**Fix:** "Wire-rimmed glasses perpetually slipping down, oversized cardigans, coffee-stained sleeves"

### ❌ Generated Scene: Doesn't match other scenes (continuity broken)
**Cause:** No continuity notes carry forward props, costume state, or emotional state.
**Fix:** "Maya still holding the envelope. Her eyes red-rimmed from recent crying."

---

## Refinement Process

If Storymaker imports but generated visuals don't match vision:

1. **Review the Production Notes** — Are they specific enough?
2. **Add More Visual Detail** — "Harsh light" → "Harsh overhead fluorescent creating shadow under jaw"
3. **Specify Continuity** — Carry props, costume state, emotional residue forward
4. **Break Shots Smaller** — 5 shots per scene minimum; 7-9 is better
5. **Test One Scene** — Export and review first scene before finalizing entire screenplay
